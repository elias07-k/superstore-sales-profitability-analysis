# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to evaluate business performance across product categories, regions, customer segments, discounts, and time periods. Using SQL and Power BI, I investigated sales trends, profitability drivers, and areas of potential business improvement.

## Tools Used
- SQL (SQLite)
- Power BI Desktop
- Excel / CSV

## Key Metrics
- **Total Sales:** $2.30M
- **Total Profit:** $286.40K
- **Total Orders:** 5,009
- **Profit Margin:** 12.47%

## Key Business Insights
- **Technology was the strongest category:** Generated approximately $836K in sales and $145K in profit.
- **Furniture had weak profitability:** Despite generating approximately $742K in sales, its profit margin was only about 2.49%.
- **Tables and Bookcases were loss-making:** Both furniture subcategories generated negative total profit.
- **Discounting was associated with lower profitability:** Higher discount tiers frequently showed negative profit, particularly at 30% and above.
- **Regional performance varied:** The West had the strongest overall profit margin, while Central had the weakest.
- **Sales grew over time:** Sales increased between 2014 and 2017, while average order value declined, suggesting growth was driven more by order volume.
- **Customer segments differed in profitability:** Consumer generated the most revenue, while Home Office had the highest profit margin.

## Dashboard Features
The Power BI dashboard includes:
- KPI cards for sales, profit, orders, and profit margin
- Sales by category
- Sales by customer segment
- Profit by subcategory
- Profit by region
- Annual sales trend
- Sales by state map
- Profit margin by category
- Interactive region and year slicers

The goal of this project was to practice SQL-based business analysis, identify trends and profitability issues, and communicate findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
